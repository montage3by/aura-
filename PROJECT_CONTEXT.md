## 4. Рынок и стратегия продаж

### Hemaya (подробно: `docs/hemaya.md`)
Обязательная программа RAK Police / GRA для всех объектов с коммерческой лицензией в RAK. Архив 90 дней, беспроводные камеры запрещены, минимум 2 наружные камеры, ИБП, изолированная сеть, обязательный договор обслуживания. Категории Basic и Advanced (Advanced: NVR/VMS, 2 МП+, IP66 и ИК снаружи, WDR на входах и монтаж не выше 2,5 м, LPR на въездах, камеры на кассах со звуком). Работать могут только аккредитованные интеграторы.

### Формат и правила
- Простой английский, короткие абзацы. EN для публикации + RU перевод, **всегда вместе**, весь пост одним сообщением (Антон публикует с iPhone).

**Basic or Advanced (05.11), EN:**

Basic or Advanced: which Hemaya category is your business in?

Yesterday I shared the Hemaya checklist for commercial sites in Ras Al Khaimah. That list is the minimum. For some businesses the requirements go further, and it depends on your category.

You are most likely in the Advanced category if you run:

✅️ a hotel, resort or hotel apartments
✅️ a mall or a supermarket with 6 or more shops or entrances
✅️ a bank, an exchange or a gold shop
✅️ a petrol station or a factory
✅️ a hospital or a polyclinic
✅️ a school or a university

Here is what Advanced adds on top of the basic list:

👉🏼 A digital system only.
The recorder has to be an NVR or a VMS. An older DVR setup, even a working one, does not meet the standard.
👉🏼 Better cameras everywhere.
2 MP or higher, recording 1080p on every channel. Outdoor cameras have to be weatherproof (IP66), vandal-proof and have infrared for night.
👉🏼 Entrances built for identification.
WDR is required, so a face stays readable against bright sunlight in the doorway. The camera is mounted no higher than 2.5 m and pointed at the door, not at the ceiling.
👉🏼 Counters and control room.
Service counters need a camera that sees both the customer and the staff, with audio. The control room needs its own camera and access control, plus operator and maintenance logs.

❌️ The most common mistake.
A business assumes it is Basic and installs a basic system. Then it turns out the site is Advanced, and part of the system has to be redone.

❓ How to check your category:
• Look at the activity on your trade licence
• Count the shops or entrances if you run a supermarket
• If you are not sure, ask before buying equipment, not after

At AURA we check the category before we design anything.

**Пожарный инспектор (версия с правками Антона), EN:**

Before AURA, my job was to walk into buildings and facilities and find what could go wrong.

From 2002 to 2010 I was a senior engineer and state inspector at Civil Defence fire supervision in Novy Urengoy, in the north of Russia. Day to day it was fire risk assessment across the city and oil and gas facilities. As a team member I also responded to large fires, from residential buildings to oil and gas facilities.

This week I wrote about Hemaya inspections, and it reminded me how an inspector actually thinks.

Every building, the same few questions:

👉🏼 What can happen here?
Not in theory, in this building. Which door will someone use at night, where the blind corner is, what is behind the gate that really matters.
👉🏼 What stops it?
A camera, a lock, an alarm, a person. Each one has to cover a real risk, not just appear on the drawing.
👉🏼 Who checks that it still works?
This is where most systems fail. Fires rarely start with something exotic: a blocked exit, an extinguisher nobody checked. Security fails the same way: a recorder with a dead hard drive, a camera knocked out of position, a password nobody changed since installation day.

❌ The mistake I saw most often.
A door that is perfectly secure and traps people inside. Doors on escape routes with access control must release when the fire alarm goes off. It is still the first thing I check on any project.

Those eight years taught me to start with the risk and choose the equipment after. At AURA every project starts the same way.
